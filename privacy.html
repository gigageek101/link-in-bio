Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2025

1. Overview

SearchForMyLinks ("we" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

2. Data We Collect

We collect minimal data to operate our service:

  • NO Cookies: We do not use cookies for tracking
  • NO Fingerprinting: We do not create device fingerprints

3. How We Use Data

We use anonymized data only for:

  • Service optimization and uptime monitoring
  • Security and abuse prevention

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on secure servers with SSL/TLS encryption
  • Data retention: Server logs kept for 30 days, then deleted
  • We do not share data with third parties
  • Compliance: GDPR, CCPA compliant

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request what data we have collected about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of analytics
  • File a complaint with a data protection authority

7. Children

Our service is not directed to persons under 18. If we become aware of data from minors, we will delete it immediately.
